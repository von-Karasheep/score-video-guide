## Crop correction found during the later export

A first export exposed half-cut notes from the next violin staff inside two first-violin excerpts. A global increase in lower padding had preserved one `espressivo` instruction but admitted neighboring notation elsewhere. The fix was page/staff-specific crop bounds: preserve the selected staff's notes, dynamics and expression text while excluding fragments from the adjacent staff. Do not solve one crowded example by expanding every crop globally.

The corrected final movie was inspected at output seconds 10, 21, 53, 90, 143, 164, 290, 375, 412, 479, 570 and 610. The 164- and 479-second excerpts exclude the neighboring violin fragments; the 290-second excerpt retains `espressivo`. This is evidence from those actual rendered frames, not a claim of exhaustive per-frame review.

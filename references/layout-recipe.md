# Layout recipe

Read every numerical region and type size from [layout.json](../assets/layout.json). Coordinates are logical x,y,width,height on 1920×1080. Scale notation, text, lines, and boxes together by 2. Share and hash this configuration across movements.

1. Fill the background and header divider. Place the work title at logical (36,22), size 40 Medium. Measure its rendered width, leave 24 px, then place the size-28 movement name with aligned glyph baselines. Shorten redundant wording before reducing a shared title scale. Recording information is at (36,80), size 18. Draw the header rule only after measuring the credit ink, with a clear gap under the glyphs. Compare the rendered sizes with the accepted Bruckner 8 movement I; a visibly smaller title, credit, staff label, beat label, analysis line, timeline label, or performance caption fails. The legend wraps within its own area and cannot intersect the title.
2. Fill score_panel white. At (42,142), show the Chinese score label, then measure/total and meter; show beat and hierarchy separately only when supported; approximate-measure mode retains measure/meter without inventing beat activity. The label area stays within 130 px height. Fit one complete score page proportionally inside score_image, preserving original instrument names. Split source spreads into individual page assets; do not place two reduced pages beside one another. Place verified Chinese labels beside corresponding staves with at least 12 px separation from notation. Offset crowded labels with accurate leaders rather than attaching a name to another staff.
3. The analysis area contains structural position/start measure, then title, then body. Default body size is 25 with 34 px leading; heading size 30 with 38 px leading. Wrap by measured glyph widths. Shorten or split cards in adjacent time windows rather than repeatedly shrinking text. Identify the actual instrument and material.
4. Use a white motif card with 16 px padding, size-27 heading, and size-20 source label. Its excerpt region runs approximately y+85 to y+172. Paste the excerpt in the same pixel scale as the card, inside that band. Do not mix a doubled origin with an undoubled slot height. Every staff of the named figure stays complete; a lower staff cut by the card edge fails even when the figure is the right one. The 2026-11-07 Shostakovich card still has that cut and is not an approved placement. Fit authentic notation proportionally and keep its edition/page/measure/staff identity correct. Distinguish current material from a prototype recollection. Keep motif letters outside notation and instrument names.
5. The listening cue is specific, size 25, at most two lines. Citations use size 18. Neither may overlap motif or PiP. Fit the performance proportionally within its box. Gesture text appears only during evidence-backed windows.
6. Apply the [form timeline rules](layout-and-render.md#timeline-opening-and-references). Color the pale track and elapsed fill by section, then draw the permanent dividers, ticks and labels above them. Compute section widths and fill from the declared clock, clamped to the track bounds. Use fixed rows for secondary material, measure range and mm:ss start time. For crowded subdivisions, retain their letters and ticks and give details in the time index.
7. Tint the current measure very lightly. Cursor width is 2.5 logical px. Use the same score transform for every overlay and verify it on a known head. Switch systems without a reverse sweep.
8. Opening identity and form pages are each 3 s. Combined works have one opening; preserve live inter-movement continuity. Separate movement files can have their own opening/end pages. Bibliography follows the decay, at least size 24 logical px; add pages rather than forcing unreadably small text. Read [covers](cover.md) separately.

Inspect encoded frames reduced to 1920×1080: unobstructed title, complete staves/labels, readable prose/excerpts, motif labels clear of instrument names, PiP clear of motif content, uncrossed timeline labels, no decimal timestamps, and no old header clock/dash. Compare region boundaries against the configuration, not a vague resemblance.

Work-specific wording, staff counts, and score proportions may vary. The type hierarchy, regions, and required modules remain consistent. Resolve missing fonts/materials rather than silently substituting them. Reference images show style, not approved historical timing. [layout-map.svg](../assets/layout-map.svg) is an inspection diagram, not a decorative overlay for the video.

Production-status banners and provisional-page-range footers are excluded from the video. Keep those details in the companion review record and reply, including for user-authorized review exports.
